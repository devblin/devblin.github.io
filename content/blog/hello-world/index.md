I have meant to start a blog for years. The usual things got in the way — the
perfect setup, the perfect first post, the perfect excuse. This is me getting
out of my own way.

No grand mission statement. This is just a place to think out loud about the
things I spend my days on: building software, the tools I reach for, the
mistakes I keep making, and the occasional thing that actually works.

Roughly, expect:

- Notes from whatever I am building
- Short write-ups of problems that took me too long to solve
- Opinions I will probably disagree with later

If any of that sounds interesting, stick around. If not, that is fine too — I am
mostly writing this for myself.

Onward.
